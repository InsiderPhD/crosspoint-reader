Install a theme

Pick a theme folder (or a .zip of one) containing theme.html and its fonts and images. Your browser lays it out, bakes everything static into a black-and-white background, and keeps the data-var elements live: the device fills them with your current stats every time it sleeps. Use a desktop Chrome or Firefox.

        Writing a theme
        

          theme.html is laid out at the screen size (the page is the screen). Any CSS works for the static
          parts. Mark live parts with attributes; their content is only a preview sample.
        

        
    
          
  • data-var="stats.streak" - one value. Add data-format="000" to zero-pad numbers or
            data-format="dd.MM.yyyy HH:mm" for dates (Unicode patterns: MM is the month, mm minutes).
    • 
          
  • data-template="Your {{stats.books_finished}}th book" - mixed text and values.
    • 
          
  • Expressions inside {{ }}: {{stats.streak == 1 ? "day" : "days"}},
            {{round(stats.week_minutes / 60)}}, {{book.title || "Nothing open"}}. Operators
            + - * / % == != < <= > >= && || ! ?: and round floor ceil abs min max upper
            lower pad plural date.
    • 
          
  • icon("star") in an expression draws icons/star.svg from the theme folder as part of
            the text: {{stats.week_met_1 ? icon("star-filled") : icon("star")}}.
    • 
          
  • data-digit="0" - only the nth digit from the right (flip-board tiles).
    • 
          
  • data-var="book.cover" on an <img> or <div> - the open
            book's cover. object-fit: contain shows all of it, cover zooms it to fill the box
            and crops the overflow, anything else stretches it. recent.2.cover is a recent
            book's; sleep.image is a random image from /sleep (or /sleep.bmp).
    • 
          
  • data-filter="grey" on an image - drawn in the screen's four greys (keep live text off it);
            otherwise it becomes black and white: dither (default), lighter,
            darker, bw (hard black and white) or invert. Rotated images and
            overlay themes always dither.
    • 
          
  • data-kind="bar" - fills the box from the left by a 0-100 value.
    • 
          
  • data-max-lines="2", data-overflow="ellipsis", data-hide-if-empty,
            data-glyphs="digits" (character sets to include for live text: latin, digits, greek,
            cyrillic, kana, cjk, hangul; default latin).
    • 
          
  • data-valign="bottom" (or middle, top) - a text box that keeps room for
            all of its data-max-lines and places shorter text at its bottom or middle, like a slide's text
            box. A title above an author stays put whether it wraps or not.
    • 
          
  • data-fit on live text with a border or background (a tag pill) - the device draws the
            border and background to fit the real text, up to the element's max-width, instead of
            keeping the sample's size. Use a width: fit-content box on its own line, or put the pills
            in a display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap parent: the device then lines them up and wraps them
            itself (data-max-lines on the parent caps the rows).
    • 
          
  • Live text and bars are drawn on top of everything static. Static art in front of an image (a card
            over a full-screen cover) stays in front: the device draws it again after the image.
    • 
          
  • Live text keeps its font, size, weight, colour (black or white), alignment, letter-spacing,
            text-transform and any 2D transform (rotate, scale, skew).
    • 
          
  • <html data-kp-orientation="landscape"> for a landscape theme;
            data-kp-dither="threshold" for crisp line art instead of dithering;
            data-kp-overlay to paint over the page that was showing - anything left transparent
            (no background on body) shows the page.
